📐 RATIO SPREAD

Range: +
📐 RATIO SPREAD P&L FORMULA
Call P&L = max(S − K_sell, 0) × (−N) + max(S − K_buy, 0) × (+M)
Put P&L = max(K_sell − S, 0) × (−N) + max(K_buy − S, 0) × (+M)
Net Debit = (M × LTP_buy) − (N × LTP_sell)
Where: S = Spot at expiry | K = Strike price | M:N = Buy:Sell ratio (e.g., 1:3) | LTP = Last Traded Price
Max Profit: (K_sell − K_buy) − Net Debit  |  Max Loss: Net Debit  |  Breakeven: K_buy + Net Debit
CALL RATIO (1:3)
PUT RATIO (1:3)

🦋 BUTTERFLY P&L FORMULA
Call BF (a:b:c) = CE(ITM)×(+a) + CE(ATM)×(−b) + CE(OTM)×(+c)
Put BF (a:b:c) = PE(ITM)×(+a) + PE(ATM)×(−b) + PE(OTM)×(+c)
Net Debit = (a × LTP_itm) − (b × LTP_atm) + (c × LTP_otm)
Where: a = Long ITM lots | b = Short ATM lots | c = Long OTM lots
Strikes: ITM = ATM − gap | OTM = ATM + gap (gap = strike interval)
Max Profit: (Wing Width × max(0, b−a−c)) − Net Debit  |  Max Loss: Net Debit
Breakevens: ITM + Net Debit | OTM − Net Debit
CALL BUTTERFLY (1:4:3)
PUT BUTTERFLY (1:4:3)
